Opening Glyphvane for the first time
====================================

1. Drag Glyphvane into the Applications folder.
2. Open it once. macOS blocks it, because Glyphvane is not notarized by Apple.
3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click
   "Open Anyway" next to the message about Glyphvane (the button is shown for
   about an hour after the blocked attempt), then confirm.

Alternatively, run this in Terminal after copying the app:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Glyphvane.app
