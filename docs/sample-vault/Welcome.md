# Welcome to the sample vault

This small vault is a guided tour of what Glyphvane can show. Every note
below covers one feature area in a few lines: read it in the Reading view,
then switch to Edit (**Ctrl+E**) or Split to see the Markdown behind it.

> [!tip] How to move around
> Click a link to follow it, and press **Alt+Left** to come back. Rest the
> pointer on a link for a preview. **Ctrl+O** finds any note by name.

## The tour

1. [[Links and backlinks]]: wikilinks, heading and block links, and the
   backlinks panel.
2. [[Embeds]]: showing one note (or a part of it) inside another.
3. [[Callouts]]: boxes for tips and warnings, some of them foldable.
4. [[Math]]: formulas, with macros of your own.
5. [[Code]]: highlighted code blocks.
6. [[Diagrams]]: flowcharts and more, drawn from text.
7. [[Tasks]]: checklists.
8. [[Tags and properties]]: front matter and `#tags`.
9. [[Tables]]: aligned columns.
10. [[Footnotes]]: notes at the end of the page.
11. [[2026-11-19|A daily note]]: one note per day.
12. [[Search queries]]: live search results inside a note.
13. [[Bases]]: a table of notes, built from their properties.
14. [[Canvas]]: a board of cards and arrows.
15. [[PDFs and images]]: attachments.

## Things to try

- Open the right panel (View ▸ Toggle right panel) and look at the
  **Outline** and the **Backlinks** of this note.
- Press **Ctrl+Shift+F** and search for `tag:#tour`.
- Open the **Tags** panel to see the tags of the vault as a tree.
- Run *Check links in the vault* from the command palette (**Ctrl+P**):
  it finds exactly one broken link, left in [[Links and backlinks]] on
  purpose.

Everything in this vault is plain text (except the picture and the PDF), so
feel free to edit, break and repair it.
