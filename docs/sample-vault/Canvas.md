# Canvas

A canvas (`.canvas` file) is a board of cards joined by arrows. Open
[[Tour board.canvas]] on its own, or look at it embedded here. Back to
[[Welcome]].

![[Tour board.canvas]]

## What is on the board

- **Text cards** with Markdown, a callout with a formula, a task list and a
  search query.
- **File cards** for the [[Welcome]] note, for only the *Macros* section of
  [[Math]], for an image and for a PDF.
- A **link card** for a web address (nothing is downloaded).
- Two **groups**, *Start here* and *Notes and parts of notes*, with labels
  and colours.
- **Arrows** with labels, colours, an arrowhead at each end, and one with
  no arrowhead at all.

In the canvas tab, **Tab** moves from card to card, **Enter** opens the
selected card, and **Shift+1** fits the whole board in the window. Canvases
are read-only in Glyphvane: edit them in Obsidian or in a text editor (they
are JSON). Back to [[Links and backlinks]] for more on links.
