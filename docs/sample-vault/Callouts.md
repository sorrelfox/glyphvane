# Callouts

A callout is a quote whose first line names a type in brackets. The type sets
the colour and the icon. Back to [[Welcome]].

> [!note]
> A plain note. Without a title, the type's name is used.

> [!tip] A title of your own
> Anything after the brackets becomes the title.

> [!warning] Mind the gap
> Callouts hold any Markdown: **bold**, `code`, [[Math|links]] and lists.
>
> - one
> - two

## Folding

A minus sign after the brackets makes a callout that starts closed; a plus
sign, one that starts open. Click the title to fold or unfold it.

> [!faq]- Why is this one closed?
> Because its type is followed by `-`. Long details, spoilers and answers
> fit well in a folded callout.

> [!example]+ This one starts open
> Its type is followed by `+`, so you can still fold it.
